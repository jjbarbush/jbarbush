Cannes Lions 2017 · Straight 8 Super 8 Film Competition

Sharkman at Cannes.

As part of our agency trip to Cannes in 2017, we assembled a small team to enter the Straight 8 Super 8 film competition. Everything was shot and edited in-camera and the undeveloped film was sent to be mixed with a soundtrack we provided. We never saw the final piece until it aired live at the 2017 Cannes Lions.
