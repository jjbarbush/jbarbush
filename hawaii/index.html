Before you go

  • Card only at every state park. Hāpuna and Waialea run on QR codes and card readers. There is nobody to take your cash.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen is state law. Anything with oxybenzone or octinoxate is banned. Zinc, a rash guard, or long sleeves.
  • Ten feet from honu. Green sea turtles are protected. Float still and let them come to you, and never get between one and the surface.
  • Morning is the whole game. Trades pick up late morning, chop the surface, and drop visibility by half. A 7 a.m. snorkel and a 2 p.m. snorkel at the same beach are two different days.
  • Check the surf before Hāpuna. It is a sand beach with a real shore break when a swell is running. Waves over three feet mean stay out.
  • Gear. Ocean Sports rents masks, fins, and paddleboards on A-Bay, which saves the drive to a Kona rental shop.
