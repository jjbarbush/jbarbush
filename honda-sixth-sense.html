Honda Sixth Sense

Honda's main objective has always been providing safe transportation. For April Fools, we created Honda Sixth Sense, a fake mobile app designed to alert pedestrians to threats. The imaginary app racked up over 2.5 million hits on YouTube and 350,000 views on Instagram, and caught the attention of BuzzFeed, Huffington Post, and The Drum.
