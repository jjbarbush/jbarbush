The Orpheum

Est. 1926
  • Opened February 15, 1926 as the fourth and final L.A. home of the Orpheum vaudeville circuit. Named for Orpheus, the Greek god of music.
  • Beaux Arts facade, French-inspired interior with gilded ceilings, two huge chandeliers, and gold-leaf detailing. The most fully restored of the old Broadway movie palaces.
  • Home to a 1928 "Mighty Wurlitzer" pipe organ, one of only three left in Southern California and still played today.
  • Past stage: a young Judy Garland, the Marx Brothers, Jack Benny, Lena Horne, Ella Fitzgerald, Duke Ellington, and later Little Richard and Aretha Franklin.

Joe Jackson

Hope & Fury Tour
  • English singer-songwriter and pianist, breakout in 1979 with "Is She Really Going Out with Him?" Best known for "Steppin' Out" and a catalog that runs new wave, jazz, swing, and classical.
  • This stop supports his newest album, "Hope and Fury," out now. Expect new material woven in with the classics.
  • Touring as Joe Jackson + Band. Recent setlists have included Steppin' Out, Real Men, Sunday Papers, Be My Number Two, and a cover of Bowie's "Scary Monsters."
  • Doors at 7 PM, show at 8 PM sharp. He plays often, but L.A. shows are rare. Worth being in your seat on time.
